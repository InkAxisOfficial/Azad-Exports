About Us

Built on one promise — quality, in every consignment.

Azad Exports is a proprietorship-run merchant export house based in Kandukur, Prakasam District, Andhra Pradesh — a region known for its rice, groundnut and vegetable cultivation. Founded by Shaik Azad, the company is newly registered with India's export authorities and built from day one on full regulatory compliance rather than shortcuts.

We source directly from local growers and processors, so every shipment we quote is backed by verifiable certification — not just a promise.
